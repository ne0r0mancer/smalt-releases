# Smalt

**Dictation that listens back.** A Mac app for dictation, meeting notes and live answers, transcribed on your Mac.

- **Dictation:** hold ⌥, talk, let go. Your words appear in any app, and your clipboard is put back.
- **Meeting notes:** from your mic and your Mac's audio, with no bot joining the call. A summary, decisions and action items, saved as Markdown.
- **Live answers:** when someone asks a question on a call, a card shows the answer from folders you choose. Built in with your own keys, or through Claude Code and Claude Desktop over a local MCP server.

Speech is recognized on the Neural Engine (Parakeet v3, 25 European languages). Audio is never saved or uploaded. No account.
